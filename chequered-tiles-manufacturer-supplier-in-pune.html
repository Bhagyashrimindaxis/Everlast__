Chequered Tiles in Pune

Ever slipped on a wet ramp? Most of us have, and it usually happens where the floor looked fine. Chequered Tiles exist for exactly these spots. They’re precast concrete tiles with a grid of raised squares on top, so shoes and trolley wheels get something to hold on to.

The common pattern is the 9 square tile, a three by three grid on each piece. You’ll see it on walkways, ramps, parking lots, pool decks, petrol pumps and industrial flooring, in plain colours and two-tone mixes.

What is Chequered Tile?

A chequered tile is a concrete floor tile made for outdoor use. The body is heavy-duty concrete, the top has a textured mosaic finish, and the face carries that raised nine-square pattern. Sizes are simple: 12 x 12 inches across and 25 mm thick. They’re made in India and precast, so they turn up ready to lay and don’t need to cure on site.

The finish is sold as anti-skid, which is a fair description. The texture is there for grip first and looks second. A flat concrete slab can get slick, and a raised grid breaks that up. You can also choose from several colours, so the floor can match the site. Here is what you can get:

  • Bright yellow: One strong colour that stands out clearly. It suits walkways, ramps, parking lots and pool decks.
  • Red and yellow: Two-tone and easy to notice. People use it for pavements, parks, petrol pumps and industrial floors.
  • Grey and black: The quieter two-tone option, used in the same kinds of places when you want the floor to blend in.
  • Red and grey: Plain red square tiles and a grey outdoor tile with a mosaic finish.

Why Choose Chequered Tiles?

Smooth tiles look clean on day one, but they can turn slippery as soon as it rains. A chequered surface gives people more confidence to walk. That alone is a good reason, though there are others. Using one tile style across a project also keeps ordering simple and the finished site consistent:

  • Traction: Raised squares plus a mosaic top. Wet, dusty or oily ground is less of a worry.
  • Thickness: At 25 mm, they feel solid and handle regular traffic outdoors.
  • Colour coding: Yellow shows up from far away. Grey and black fade into the background.
  • Easy maths: Every tile is 12 x 12 inches, so counting how many you need is quick and ordering is simple.
  • Upkeep: No repainting. Sweep it, wash it, done. The colours stay clear with regular cleaning.
  • Swapping: One cracked tile can be lifted out and replaced without touching its neighbours.
  • Range of use: The same tile can go in a park, beside a pool or in a factory yard.

Applications of Chequered Tiles

Anywhere people walk on an open-air floor that gets wet or dirty. A petrol pump sees drivers in a hurry, a pool deck sees barefoot kids and a factory yard sees workers carrying loads. These are the usual places:

  • Walkways and pavements: Garden paths, footpaths and approach roads. Damp mornings are where grip matters.
  • Ramps: A slope is harder to walk on, especially with a trolley or a wheelchair. The texture helps, and a yellow strip along the edge is easy to spot.
  • Parking lots: Open parking bays and lanes need a flat, firm surface. A level, compacted base keeps the tiles even.
  • Swimming pool decks: The ground is wet for hours, so a slip-resistant top is a must.
  • Petrol pumps: Spills, tyres and people moving around all day. A tough, textured tile makes sense here.
  • Industrial flooring: Yards, loading areas and the paths beside machinery. A red-yellow border can show where the pedestrian path ends and the vehicle lane begins.
  • Public parks: Children, older visitors and joggers all use the same paths, so safety and looks both count.

Salient Features Chequered Tiles

What sets these tiles apart is fairly simple, and most of it is visible the moment you pick one up. Here’s a quick list:

  • Heavy-duty concrete: Dense and solid, not a light decorative material.
  • Mosaic top: The textured finish is what gives the tile its anti-skid grip, even when the surface is damp.
  • 25 mm thick: Enough body for outdoor and industrial jobs, where thin tiles can crack.
  • 12 x 12 inch square: Easy to carry, cut and lay.
  • The 9 square pattern: A neat grid that looks the same across a large floor.
  • Colours: Yellow, red-yellow, grey-black, red and grey, so most sites have something that fits.
  • Two-tone options: Bolder or calmer, depending on the site and who will be walking there.
  • Factory-made: Precast tiles keep their size and finish consistent from one batch to the next, so a big floor looks even.
  • Made in India: Built for open-air use.

Benefits Chequered Tiles

A floor is something you only notice when it goes wrong, which is why it deserves some thought. The right tile keeps it from going wrong, and that matters for owners, contractors and people who simply walk across it. Here’s what you gain:

  • Fewer slips: Grip on wet or oily ground protects visitors, staff and customers, and that means fewer accidents to deal with.
  • Low upkeep: You won’t be resurfacing or repainting. Regular cleaning is enough.
  • Lasting use: A thick tile copes with weather, footfall and light vehicles, year after year.
  • Simple zoning: Colours mark walkways, ramps and caution areas, so you need less paint and fewer signs to guide people.
  • Tidy laying: Same-size tiles go down quickly and look even, which saves time on site.
  • Clear guidance: Bold colours help people find routes and see edges in busy areas.
  • Fewer complaints: When people feel safe on a floor, they stop talking about the floor. That’s the best result you can hope for.
  • Value over time: A tough tile that needs little care can cost less in the long run than a cheaper floor that wears out early.
