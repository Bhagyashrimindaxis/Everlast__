Concrete Tree Grating / Guards

Precast Concrete Tree Gratings & Tree Guards

Engineered for sustainable urban forestry and green infrastructure, our Tree Grating Guard collection features durable, porous designs that protect root zones while maximizing space for pedestrian traffic. These precast matrices allow rainwater and nutrients to filter directly down to the roots, preventing soil compaction and supporting healthy tree growth along public footpaths, parks, and city avenues.

Tree Grating / Gaurd

Thickness: 80 mm

Material: Heavy-Duty Reinforced Precast Concrete

Color: Concrete Grey / Charcoal Black

Shape: Square Outer Frame with Circular Trunk Cutout

Brand: Everlast

Inner Diameter: 450 mm / 600 mm (Standard Tree Clearance)
