Rough Finishing Pavers in Pune

Anyone who has stepped onto a wet ramp in the monsoon knows that small, unpleasant jolt when a shoe starts to slide. Outdoor ground in Pune puts up with rain, oil drips, tyre traffic and thousands of footsteps every day, and the finish on top decides whether people feel steady or wary. Rough finishing pavers are made for exactly that problem.

Everlast has been making paver blocks, kerb stones and manhole covers from Manjari Budruk, Pune, since 2009. Our Rough Finish collection includes a heavy-duty concrete paver and a cement paver, both built around a 200 × 200 mm face with a textured top that grips under foot and tyre.

A paver is easy to underrate until the ground starts to fail. What Is Rough Finishing Paver?

A rough finish paver is a precast concrete block whose top face is left deliberately textured instead of being smoothed or polished. The texture creates friction. Shoes, wheels and walking sticks find something to hold on to, even when the surface is damp. Because each block is made in a controlled setting and laid by hand, you also get a tidy, even surface without pouring a single slab. Individual blocks can be lifted later if a utility line needs access, which a poured slab cannot offer.

Everlast offers two versions in this collection:

  • Rough Finish Paver Block: Made from a heavy-duty concrete matrix with a high anti-skid grip. It comes in 200 × 200 mm, 60 mm or 80 mm thick, in square or rectangular shapes, and in grey, terracotta red or yellow.
  • Rough Finish Cement Paver Block: Made from a high-grade cement concrete matrix with a premium matte, skid-resistant texture. It comes in 200 × 200 mm or custom sizes, 60 mm or 80 mm thick, in square or interlocking profiles, and in natural grey, terracotta red, yellow or charcoal black.

Applications Of Rough Finishing Paver

The Rough Finish range is built for places where grip and strength both matter. Typical uses include:

  • Public ramps: A textured face helps pedestrians and wheelchair users keep their footing on a slope.
  • Commercial driveways: The blocks cope with regular vehicle movement and still look neat at the entrance.
  • Parking lots: Large paved areas need a surface that handles tyres, drips and constant turning.
  • Fuel stations: Oil and fuel spills are a hazard, so a skid-resistant texture is a sensible choice here.
  • Heavy pedestrian walkways: Malls, campuses and public paths see crowds, and the surface needs to wear evenly.
  • Residential and landscaped areas: Gate approaches, garden paths and open courtyards can use the same blocks in softer colours.

Every site is a little different. A shaded lane beside a canteen has different needs from an open fuel station forecourt, so it helps to describe the layout, the drainage and the expected traffic. If you are unsure which thickness or shape fits, our team will help you decide.

Benefits Of Rough Finishing Paver

Picking the right paver pays off long after the job is finished:

  • Safer surfaces: Better grip means fewer slips for people and less skidding for vehicles, especially in wet conditions.
  • Low upkeep: A hardwearing concrete block needs little more than occasional cleaning, so the running cost stays small over the years.
  • Easy repair: If one block cracks or stains, it can be lifted and replaced without redoing the whole area. That saves both money and disruption.
  • A good look: Consistent colour and neat joints give the finished ground a clean, planned appearance.
  • Responsible production: Everlast works with sustainable materials and aims to keep waste low.
