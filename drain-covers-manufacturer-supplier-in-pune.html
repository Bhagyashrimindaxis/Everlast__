Drain Covers in Pune

Nobody plans a day around a drain, yet a bad one can ruin it. A missing or cracked cover is a trip hazard on a footpath, a danger to tyres on a road, and a way for rubbish and soil to clog the channel underneath. A good cover simply sits flush, carries the load and lets the water do its work. That’s why the cover matters as much as the drain itself.

Everlast, based in Manjari Budruk, Pune, makes concrete drain covers in two forms: a compact 200 x 200 x 60 mm square cover, and a heavy-duty precast cover that sits on an integrated U-trough. We’ve been manufacturing and supplying concrete products since 2009, with 500+ projects behind us, and we supply across India. Whether you’re finishing a housing society’s perimeter or a stormwater line along a highway, we can help you choose the right cover, and we’re happy to advise on how many you need for a run and how they should be laid.

What Is Drain Cover?

A drain cover is a slab that closes the top of a drain or trench. It keeps people and vehicles from falling in, keeps debris out and lets the channel below carry water away. In our range, that looks like this:

  • A compact square cover: It measures 200 x 200 x 60 mm, comes in grey concrete and is made for outdoor use.
  • A heavy-duty trough cover: It’s a flush-fitting slab designed to sit inside the recessed lips of a U-trough, so the cover and the channel work as one system.
  • Reinforced concrete: The heavy-duty cover uses high-strength reinforced precast concrete in an M30/M40 grade matrix.
  • Precast in a factory: Each piece is cast before it reaches your site, which keeps edges and sizes consistent.
  • Natural grey finish: The plain concrete look suits roads, industrial yards and residential edges alike.
  • Made in India: Every cover carries the Everlast brand and is made in India.

A cover is only as good as the trench it sits on. Straight, level lips and a clear channel let the slabs seat properly, and our team is happy to talk through the details before you order.

Applications of Drain Covers

Anywhere water has to be moved, a drain cover keeps the route safe. Common places include:

  • Highway and roadside drainage: Heavy-duty covers close the trough beside the road while carrying surface loads. They keep the channel clear and the road edge safe for traffic.
  • Industrial stormwater systems: Factories and yards need drains that cope with trucks and equipment. A strong precast cover stands up to that traffic.
  • Commercial townships: Large campuses have long drain runs along roads and walkways. Matching covers keep the line neat and safe.
  • Residential perimeters: Society boundaries and compound walls often have a drain along the edge. A cover keeps it tidy and stops debris collecting.
  • Trench enclosures: Open trenches for cables or water are hazards. A cover turns them into a flat, usable surface.
  • Parking and loading areas: Drains run through yards and bays where vehicles move constantly. A solid cover keeps the line safe and level.
  • Footpaths and open spaces: The compact square cover suits lighter outdoor areas where a small drain opening needs closing. It sits neatly in the surface and stays out of the way.
